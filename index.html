2 Transcribe

Two engines, same editor. Pick one, then press the big button.

Your key stays in your browser (saved only in this browser’s local storage) and is sent only to Groq. Get a free key at console.groq.com → API Keys.

How to get your FREE Groq API key (takes 1 minute — no card needed):
  1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign up / log in (free — Google or email).
  2. Click Create API Key, give it any name (e.g. “srt”), and submit.
  3. Copy the key — it starts with gsk_ and is shown only once.
  4. Paste it in the box above. Done — it is saved in your browser, so next time you will not need it again.

Files bigger than 24 MB are automatically converted to small 16 kHz audio and sent in ~10-minute parts. Free Groq limits are generous (thousands of requests per day), but if you hit one, the tool will tell you in plain words.

The model downloads once and stays cached in your browser — after that this engine works fully offline and costs nothing, forever. Tiny and Base models are intentionally not offered: they fail badly on Hindi/Urdu. Transcription happens in a background worker, so the page never freezes. Best on a desktop/laptop.
